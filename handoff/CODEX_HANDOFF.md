# glmDev.com — project vision and decisions

## Positioning
A professional software engineer's personal portfolio combined with a boutique software/AI engineering consultancy. This site sells the engineer's expertise, in contrast to the more business-benefit-oriented TheAINerd.com. It should be concise and visually led, with roughly half the verbosity of the latter. Primary goal: encourage qualified custom software, integration, automation, AI engineering, and consulting inquiries.

## Approved aesthetic
Primary: `design/APPROVED_HOMEPAGE_REFERENCE.png`. Clean white background, near-black/navy typography, vibrant blue CTAs, light blue feature bands, soft rounded cards, illustrative dashboard/laptop in hero. The final full-page mockup includes the new AI consulting/training section between services and engineer bio. The detail `design/AI_TRAINING_SECTION_DETAIL.png` shows an expanded layout with four offerings; use that for substance, but match the overall page's restrained density. No need to copy mockup wording letter-for-letter; user explicitly said copy can be refined later.

## Key choices
- Keep exactly **six** service cards in two rows of three on desktop.
- **AI Consulting & Training** gets its own prominent, visually distinct horizontal feature section; do not add a seventh card.
- The owner and another collaborator may offer group AI instruction; avoid describing the collaborator as an employee, named partner, or formally established team without approval.
- Friendly IT support clarification: not general printer/computer/Wi-Fi troubleshooting, but happy to refer someone to a capable IT provider.
- Experience: 10+ years in software engineering, software engineer since 2016. Do not invent employment dates, qualifications, or project statistics.
- Site technology: static HTML/CSS/JS + minimal PHP/PHPMailer for contact. Bluehost shared hosting. No Laravel. No third-party form service. No GitHub required.
- Contact form is the only server-side function. SMTP mailbox, host, ports, and secrets are to be supplied/configured by owner later.

## Navigation and structure
Logo glmDev; Home, Services, Work, Experience, About, Contact / Let's Connect. Single-page anchors are acceptable; Experience can be a separate page later. Hero -> services -> AI consulting/training -> engineer bio + technology -> work -> IT support note -> contact -> footer.

## Technology claims
.NET/C#, Angular, SQL Server, TypeScript, Python, REST APIs, Azure/AWS, AI/LLMs. Treat cloud providers as areas of experience, not invented certifications. The owner works full-time and is available for select independent projects. Portfolio samples should be anonymized and not expose confidential employer/customer data.

## Avoid
Excessive copy, inflated agency claims, imaginary testimonials or customers, fake case study screenshots presented as real, misleading live metrics, intrusive animations, overcomplicated front-end frameworks, unnecessary databases, or a 'help desk' identity.
